STEM Engagement

Notre Dame Rocketry Outreach Team: Bringing the thrill of aerospace design and engineering discoveries directly into local classrooms and community spaces.

Our Commitment

Inspiring the Local Community

Notre Dame Rocketry's passion for STEM extends far beyond our engineering labs on campus. The team is fully committed to giving back to the local communities of South Bend and Mishawaka through our immersive STEM Engagement events.

Operated purely on a volunteer basis and supported heavily by our sponsors, these programs offer unique opportunities to meet and talk to excited young minds curious about nature, science, and technology. Every year, our student builders reach over 1,300+ community members through dynamic rocket construction modules and fluid dynamics design experiments.

Hands-On STEM Workshops

Interactive Activities

Participating schools and local community cohorts can engage with custom, interactive projects tailored closely to their specific grade groups. Our design tracks allow students to actively construct, test, and troubleshoot aerospace projects including:

Paper Helicopters Straw Rockets Stomp Rockets Filter Parachutes Paper Plate Planets Notecard Towers Spaghetti Bridges
