Our Commitment

Inspiring the Surrounding Community

Notre Dame Rocketry's passion for STEM extends far beyond our engineering labs on campus. The team is fully committed to giving back to the local communities of South Bend and Mishawaka through our high-impact STEM Engagement events.

Operated purely on a volunteer basis and heavily supported by our gracious sponsors, these interactive days provide our members with the unique opportunity to interact with excited, young minds curious about nature, science, and technical exploration.

1,300+

Community Members Reached

Every year, our student builders run custom, hands-on instructional workshops to motivate children across the Indiana region into modern engineering paths.

Curriculum

Depending on the age range, participating schools and local organizations can select from a wide variety of hands-on technical activities designed to teach fundamental physics and engineering.
