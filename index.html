About NDRT

At the Notre Dame Rocketry Team, we design, manufacture, test, and launch high-powered rockets. Our mission is to transform classroom theory into real-world engineering solutions. By developing complex aerospace technologies like custom avionics and our new experimental liquid propulsion systems, our members gain hands-on experience that goes far beyond traditional coursework. Ultimately, our goal is greater than simply achieving the perfect launch. We are dedicated to equipping the next generation of engineers with the practical skills, technical rigor, and collaborative mindset they need to lead the future of the aerospace industry.

Our Projects

We manage several concurrent engineering projects. Currently, our team is designing high-power solid vehicles for the NASA Student Launch (USLI) competition, while also developing Project NDXP—a multi-year initiative to build Notre Dame's first liquid bipropellant rocket engine.
